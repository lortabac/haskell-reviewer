| D-86 | Cheap, leak-free spawns and no stall on big responses (T07b). (1) Children get no inherited fds, cheaply: first thing in `main`, `prepareSpawning` marks every fd ≥ 3 close-on-exec (listed from `/dev/fd`, Linux and macOS), then lowers the soft `RLIMIT_NOFILE` to 4096 (hard unchanged; not lowered if `/dev/fd` can't be read or shows no fd above 2). `close_fds = True` stays on every spawn, now at most 4096 `close()` calls; children inherit the lowered soft limit. The bench harness spawns the product with `close_fds = False` (posix_spawn) and keeps its own limit, so the product sees the user's real one.
